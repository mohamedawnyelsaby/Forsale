Privacy Policy

Draft. Written from how the app works today. Items marked [ ] must be completed by the owner, and the text should be reviewed by a lawyer before launch. Last updated: 28 Nov 2026.

What we process

  • Pi account: your Pi username and a user ID, received through Pi Authentication when you tap “Connect Pi”.
  • Payments: payment ID, amount and transaction ID, handled through Pi Network.
  • Marwan (AI): the questions you type and the property being discussed are sent to third-party AI providers (Google Gemini and Groq) to generate answers. Do not type personal information.
  • Listings you create: title, city, type, price and size. In this version they are kept only in your browser session.
  • On your device: saved homes, language, theme and a “signed-in” flag (browser storage). Signing out removes the flag on this device.

What we do not do

No advertising, no analytics trackers, no selling of data.

Services we rely on

Pi Network (authentication and payments), Vercel (hosting), Google (fonts and Gemini), Groq (AI).

Your choices

You can sign out in Account, clear saved homes by un-saving them, or clear this site’s data in your browser. To ask about your data, contact [contact email].
